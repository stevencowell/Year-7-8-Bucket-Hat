NESA fit and boundary

  • Current syllabus: Technology 7–8 Syllabus (2023), implemented from 2026.
  • Focus area: Materials and production processes.
  • Course role: this is one approximately 30-hour project unit within the full Years 7–8 course.
  • Across Years 7–8: all four focus areas must be delivered; practical learning and project work must occupy most course time; students must document, communicate and evaluate design and production processes.
  • Digital boundary: TE4-DIG-01 is supported through responsible research and digital evidence. TE4-DIG-02 is not claimed unless a real teacher-approved data or coding activity is taught.

Authority boundary

  • Primary project authority: “Bucket Hat for a Cause” Stage 4 unit program and 2026 Bucket Hat outline.
  • Assessment authority: page 16 of the Year 7 Assessment Booklet 2026 for course-wide tasks, timing, outcomes and weightings; the issued task notification controls exact conditions and rubric.
  • Sequence authority: Term One and Term Two weekly Classroom lessons, including design, sampling, machine preparation, construction and evaluation.
  • Excluded conflict: older bag, lunch-bag and carry-item wording in inherited generic textile worksheets is not used as Bucket Hat project fact.
  • Private material excluded: student submissions, names, grades and student photographs were not opened or reused.

Practical limits

The website does not invent dimensions, seam allowances, material quantities, machine settings or construction details. Use the approved pattern, the actual classroom machine, teacher demonstrations and current safety training.

Videos provide conceptual support only; they do not override the approved project method.
