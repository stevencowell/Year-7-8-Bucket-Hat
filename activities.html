Project pathway

Do the real design work.

Each activity has a clear goal, source visual, ordered steps and autosaving evidence. Complete the stages your teacher directs.

0/10
Activity 1 Textiles and purpose investigation

Your goal

Recognise textiles as designed materials and define a respectful purpose for the hat.

  1. List textile products from several contexts.
  2. Study the hat-purpose gallery.
  3. Choose a possible user and cause within teacher guidance.

Save as evidence: contexts list, user/cause statement and respectful-use boundary.

Activity 2 Safety and tools audit

Your goal

Explain the controls that make textile practical work safe.

  1. Read the workstation image.
  2. Identify five hazards and controls relevant to your room.
  3. Justify four tools by function and safe use.
Hazard observedPossible harmControl used in our roomHow I know it is working

Save as evidence: hazard controls, tool justifications and stop-work triggers.

Activity 3 Visual message studio

Your goal

Turn a researched message into an original colour-and-symbol direction.

  1. Complete See–Think–Wonder.
  2. Analyse one campaign without copying it.
  3. Propose an original colour palette and symbol.

Save as evidence: visual analysis and original message direction.

Activity 4 Brief and criteria builder

Your goal

Define the context, user and need, then write a useful brief and testable criteria.

  1. Frame the situation.
  2. Write the design brief.
  3. Separate criteria from real constraints.
CriterionWhy it mattersTest methodEvidence to record

Save as evidence: situation, brief, criteria and constraints.

Activity 6 Fibre and sustainability comparison

Your goal

Compare cotton and polyester without making unsupported claims.

  1. Explain fibre → yarn → fabric.
  2. Compare properties for the actual hat use.
  3. Record one responsible material action and one evidence limitation.

Save as evidence: process explanation, property comparison and qualified sustainability judgement.

Activity 7 Decorative technique sample log

Your goal

Use supervised sample trials to select a technique that suits the fabric, user and message.

  1. Record the real materials and demonstrated steps.
  2. Evaluate each sample with observed evidence.
  3. Select a technique and name the next refinement.
Approved techniqueMaterials and demonstrated processObserved resultOne-variable refinement

Save as evidence: honest sample log and evidence-based selection.

Activity 8 Pattern and machine readiness

Your goal

Show that you can read the approved pattern and prepare for controlled machine work.

  1. Identify the symbols on the teacher-approved pattern.
  2. Write the layout, marking and cutting checks.
  3. Record real machine-practice evidence and teacher approval status.

Save as evidence: pattern-reading checks and honest readiness record.

Activity 9 Action plan and progress evidence

Your goal

Follow the approved sequence, manage quality and record real production evidence.

  1. Plan the next practical stages.
  2. Record progress after each stage.
  3. Describe one problem-solving cycle.

Activity 10 Evaluation and designer statement

Your goal

Evaluate the real hat against criteria and communicate the design journey honestly.

  1. Test each criterion using available evidence.
  2. Record self/peer feedback and your response.
  3. Draft the designer statement and identify required product photographs.
