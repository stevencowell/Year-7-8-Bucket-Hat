How the Bucket Hat unit supplies evidence

60%

Project & Practical

  • completed reversible bucket hat
  • teacher-observed safe work and practical readiness
  • accurate preparation, controlled construction and quality checks
  • honest process and final-product evidence

25%

Folio

  • research, situation, brief, criteria and constraints
  • mood board, concepts, annotations, feedback and PMI
  • material and technique trials
  • action plan, progress log, evaluation and designer statement

15%

Design knowledge

  • module theory and section learning checks
  • guided written explanations and justifications
  • materials, sustainability, design, safety and production knowledge

This evidence pathway is a teacher-program mapping, not a task-specific rubric. TE4-DIG-02 is only applicable where a real teacher-approved data or coding activity is taught.

Before you submit

  1. Use the teacher-issued task notice and exact submission directions.
  2. Check every claim against your real product, folio and practical work.
  3. Remove placeholders and clearly label incomplete or planned work.
  4. Check photographs contain no other student names, faces or private information.
  5. Download a JSON backup and print a folio copy if directed.
